# Decision-method integration gate state

`integration-gate-state.json` tracks all 19 prerequisites in `decision-method.md` §14. Its item numbers and timing follow that public method. `owner_scope` identifies the public area responsible for the artifact; it is not an approval signature. At creation, every item is `OPEN`. A draft file, planned path, blocked environment or successful test alone does not change an item to `PASS`.

Run `python research/tools/methods/gate_admission.py audit` to see the register and live checks. Run `python research/tools/methods/gate_admission.py admit --event record_admission` before committing an experiment record with decision IDs. `admit` exits nonzero while any required item is OPEN or the register is stale. `record_admission` and `every_experiment` also remain blocked until a committed experiment-to-decision assignment verifier binds the actual record to its reviewed ledger rows and refuses unresolved metric-registration blockers; a caller-supplied decision-ID flag or dictionary is not that verifier. Initial ledger assignment review at G-A may still record an explicit blocker without admitting an experiment for that decision. Admission events include `first_result`, `first_validation`, `freeze`, `decided`, and the OD-16, HC-06, remote and CC conditional events. Omitted measurement kind is treated conservatively as timing and memory. The caller that integrates these events into experiment execution must derive conditions from the registered experiment and decision record.

G-A currently reruns the exact crosswalk generator check and its focused tests, verifies every row has an independent reviewer and every F target is in objective Appendix B, compares generated objective Appendix C byte-for-byte after newline normalization, runs the strict v2 ledger `--gate-a` validator and schema tests, and validates the look registry and its tests. G-A item 10–14 remain OPEN until accepted exact-source receipts, independent review and committed outputs exist. A signed crosswalk is one component of item 10; it is not HC conformance evidence. Item 9's complete R0–R10 rule simulation is due before the first validation look and is mandatory at G-D for DEC-ECO-078; it is not a G-A prerequisite.

For implemented items 10, 11, 12 and 14, a `PASS` receipt must identify the item, governing method digest, ancestor source commit, exact artifact digests, fresh check verdict digests, and distinct producer and reviewer sessions. The review artifact must be committed, hash-matched and bind the item and exact artifact digests. Current HEAD must contain the same artifact bytes as the attested source commit. Items without a dedicated live verifier cannot become PASS by setting a status flag or supplying a nominal receipt. Future deliverables add their own verifiers and receipts in the phase fixed by §14.

An experiment result that assigns graded selection or utility weight to an OD with a reviewed no-primary disposition is refused by `graded_route_errors`. No-primary means a binary, cost, method or admission condition is governed by its cited rule; it does not authorize a late primary metric. A reviewed `metric_registration_blockers_by_od` entry identifies a missing canonical instrument, role or band for that decision: initial assignment may be reviewed at G-A, but decision-bearing preregistration, results and `DECIDED` remain blocked until the registration is resolved. The canonical band and role must be committed before the first decision-relevant result (`decision-method.md` §0.2). First-result admission remains blocked until an exact-source experiment metric-use record is integrated; a caller-supplied metric dictionary cannot promote it to PASS.

The current G-A verifier reads only public method, ledger, objective, crosswalk, look-plan, registry and test artifacts. Held-out custody items 5 and 15 remain OPEN with no verifier here. An eligible later implementation must handle public held-out reports and lock/freeze records while refusing payload and fingerprint access.
