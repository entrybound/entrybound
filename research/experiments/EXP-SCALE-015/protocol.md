## Question

On native ARM64 Linux and macOS/APFS: are pack bytes and identities identical to x86-64 Linux (PCI, LAI, PCR; AUX explained by FidelityReport for macOS-only metadata classes); do the EXP-SCALE-001 operation classes hold (memory growth with plaintext, entries); and what speedups do identity-passing parallel designs reach on ARM big/little or Apple P/E core classes?

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** identical PCR and LAI to x86-64 Linux for every small tuning/validation item; macOS AUX differences fully explained by `macos.flags`, `macos.birthtime` and resource-fork FidelityReport entries; operation classes identical to EXP-SCALE-001; parallel speedup S(n) on performance cores distinguishable from 1 for the same designs as EXP-SCALE-013.
- **H0 / falsification:** binary differences (artifacts) or class differences; speedup verdicts per §4.11.
