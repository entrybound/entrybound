## 2. Question

Consider whole tuning trees, with exact-chunk dedup archive-wide across files as in production (`entrybound::chunker::evaluate`: identical ContentObjects counted once). At matched realized mean chunk size, how do all CDC constructions under consideration compare on:

- unique stored plaintext bytes;
- chunk-size distribution (CV, forced-maximum fraction, sub-minimum tails);
- per-category dedup?

Which constructions and sizes survive to end-to-end archive measurement (EXP-CHUNK-004)?

## 3. Hypotheses

- **H1a (construction).** At corpus level and matched realized means, `gear-spread-nc2` is within the T-01 band of `gear-norm-v1` on `dedup_stored_fraction`. It is better by at least 1 band unit in at least one of F15, F16 or F20 `[INFERRED: low-bit masks shorten the effective hash window, docs/chunking-v1.md]`. **Falsified if** no family shows the favourable ≥ 1 band-unit difference, or if the corpus-level point estimate favours `gear-norm-v1` by more than the band.
- **H1b (hashless).** AE, RAM, SeqCDC and the VRAM scalar rule are worse than `gear-norm-v1` by at least 1 band unit on `dedup_stored_fraction` in at least one of F10, F15, F16 or F20, and have `forced_max_fraction` at least 2× that of `gear-norm-v1` there. **Falsified if** no such family exists.
- **H1c (size).** `dedup_stored_fraction` decreases monotonically as target size falls from 4 MiB to 16 KiB for F17 and F18 items. The frozen-constant modelled cost `modelled_cost_bytes_frozen_constants` has an interior minimum at a target at or above 64 KiB for most families. **Falsified if** the modelled cost minimum lies at the smallest grid target in most families.
- **H0.** All constructions are equivalent within the T-01 band in every family at every grid target.
- **Controls (binary; the run is invalid on failure):**
  - on items processed by both tools, `tree-dedup` output equals EXP-CHUNK-001's wrapper output for `gear-norm-v1` and the frozen presets: `unique_chunk_bytes`, `unique_chunk_count` and `manifest_chunk_references` exactly;
  - `gear-norm-v1` at the frozen presets reproduces `entrybound::chunker::evaluate`'s `estimated_cost_bytes` exactly;
  - `fixed-size` fails the EXP-CHUNK-001 edit negative control, and every CDC candidate passes it.

### Stage B (generated by `make_stage_b_spec.py`, committed before its run)

- **Incumbent size classes** (DEC-CMP-002 alternatives), each with the incumbent's own algorithm. Every parameter is read from the pinned upstream source and cited `path:line` by the implementer before the Stage B spec is generated. The values in brackets are provisional and unverified:

## 12. Analysis

- **Stage A.** Same procedure as EXP-CHUNK-001 section 12:
  - matched realized means by log-log interpolation over the 4 targets, evaluated at `gear-norm-v1`'s realized means;
  - L2-L4 with equal family weights (informational until `workload-mix.json` exists);
  - exploratory R4 (conditions 1, 2, 4) and the survivor rule.
- **Per-category analysis.** Using detail files, recompute `dedup_stored_fraction` per magic-v0 class across all items (a class is a stratum, not a family). Report the per-class best target at matched means. This is an exploratory input to EXP-CHUNK-005.
- **Stage B.** Per family and per class:
  - the epsilon-Pareto frontier (`stats.pareto_frontier`) over (`dedup_stored_fraction`, `modelled_cost_bytes_frozen_constants`, `unique_chunk_count`) for each survivor;
  - the size candidates entering EXP-CHUNK-004: the union of per-family frontier points, the four frozen presets and the incumbent size classes, capped at 12 (sizes) × survivors ≤ 4.

  The frontier is reported per family and aggregated (DEC-CMP-001 and DEC-CMP-002 "per-category and aggregate Pareto frontiers").
- **Headroom.** For each size candidate, the minimum over items of 4,000,000 / `max_chunks_in_one_object`, and the extrapolation to the 8 GiB tier bound. Any candidate at or above the limit is flagged for R2 (resource bound).
- **Labels.** All verdicts are `PRE_FREEZE`, `UNADJUSTED`, exploratory.

## 14. Sensitivity

- Matched versus unmatched means.
- Equal versus byte-weighted aggregation.
- Leave-one-family-out survivor stability.
- Tier-stratified survivors.
- Removal of F20 (adversarial) from aggregation; F20 remains an R1/R2 screening family.

## 15. Expected negative results

- Spread masks give no dedup advantage on real content, only on synthetic periodic inputs.
- Borg's 4095-byte window equals the 64-byte window within band.
- Chonkers' locality guarantee costs dedup at matched means.
- The frozen-constant modelled cost is minimized at the finest target on F17 and F18, contradicting the rationale in `docs/chunking-v1.md` that the proxy "prevents smaller chunks from winning merely because they create more dedup hits".
- Incumbent size classes (restic, Borg) dominate the frozen `balanced` preset on F18 version series.

## 16. Threats to validity

- Plaintext unique bytes ignore compression (EXP-CHUNK-004).
- The magic-v0 classifier is crude, so per-class results are exploratory.
- New algorithm ports may be unfaithful. Each needs unit tests against published examples or reference code where available, and a second-reader review of the port (recorded in the crate README) before Stage A runs.
- Incumbent parameters from recollection are replaced by cited values or the incumbent candidate is dropped.
- Detail files outside git: their SHA-256 values are in the raw rows, which keeps provenance; the files are regenerable by rerun.
- Duplicate trees (F17) are generated from the same upstreams as other families (known F03/F17 cross-family covariates, `critique-round2.md`). This is reported, not corrected.

## 18. Tooling to build

1. **`ebr-chunk tree-dedup` subcommand** (`research/harness/crates/ebr-chunk/src/tree_dedup.rs`).
   - **Flags:** `--item-path <file|dir> --algorithm <name> --min --target --max [--normalization|--window|--value-width <v|none>] --scope archive --reference-algorithm <name> --detail-out <path.gz>`.
   - **Traversal and state:** walks regular files sorted by relative path bytes, no symlink following. Streams each file, keeping only (SHA-256, length) per unique chunk plus boundary offsets per distinct object.
   - **Output fields:** the section 10 fields in one `ebr.harness.raw.v1` JSON line. `boundary_set_sha256` = SHA-256 over (object digest ‖ u64 boundary offsets), objects in digest order.
   - **Detail file:** one row per file: relpath SHA-256, size, magic-v0 class, chunk count, first-occurrence bytes, shared bytes.
   - **Validation:** held-out guard on the item path. Unit tests prove equality with `entrybound::chunker::evaluate` at the frozen presets.
2. **`gear_spread.rs`, `seqcdc.rs`, `vram_scalar.rs`, `chonkers.rs`** under `ebr-chunk/src/algorithms/`. Each carries a doc citation, unit tests (determinism, min/max enforcement, edit locality) and a size-calibration test at 4 targets.
3. **Arbitrary-size support** in `Algorithm::from_options` (not only presets); `gear-norm-v1` uses a leaked `chunker_id` string with the frozen grammar.
4. **`research/experiments/EXP-CHUNK-002/incumbent-presets.json`** with cited upstream parameters.
5. **`analyze_stage.py`** and **`make_stage_b_spec.py`** (stdlib + `ebr.stats`).
