## 1. Question

What CPU time and peak memory does each surviving cross-file candidate add at pack time (per stage
and in total), and does any stage's CPU work grow superlinearly on adversarial many-similar
inputs, so that the byte-level winners of EXP-CROSSFILE-001/-002/-003/-004/-012 can be checked
against OD-06 and OD-08 and against HC-06's complexity bound?

## 2. Hypotheses

- **H1a.** Similarity alternatives with banded LSH or content-defined sampling cost no more than
  the T-05 band (dense profile band) of `encode_cpu_s` over `sq-bottomk-v1`, while
  `oracle-exact-jaccard` and unbounded-scan variants cost more than 1 band unit.
- **H1b.** Dictionary training dominates the cohort stage for balanced; lookback candidate search
  (levels 9-19 over every lookback) dominates for extreme; COVER optimisation costs more than
  1 band unit of the extreme T-05 band.
- **H1c (HC-06, MVT-06(d)).** For `sq-bottomk-v1` the fitted exponent's 0.99 interval lower bound
  stays at or below 1 + delta (delta = 0.15) on the many-similar series because the candidate cap
  bounds leader comparisons; at least one alternative without a cap violates it.
- **H0.** No surviving candidate differs from its reference beyond the T-05 band in any stage.
- **Falsification.** H1a/H1b by the confirmatory verdicts on validation; H1c if the status quo's
  interval lower bound exceeds 1.15 (an HC-06 violation record against production).

## 10. Normalization and statistical analysis

- Item level: median of per-round paired log ratios against the reference of the same profile
  (pairs within rounds, `pairing: item_repetition`); exact order-statistic interval (§4.10).
- Corpus level: Welch-Satterthwaite; confirmatory confidences per §4.12; family harm guard at
  0.90; invalid-sample balance rules (§4.4).
- Calibration dependence: A/A, A/A', known-effect and background-load calibration for
  `time_cpu` and `memory_peak` in `wsl-ubuntu` must pass (§4.7) before any verdict here is
  decision-grade; the item-level MDE from A/A half-widths must be <= 1 band unit.
- Hostile series: least-squares slope of ln(CPU s) on ln(elements) with a 0.99 interval
  (Appendix C.1 complexity bound); violation if the lower bound exceeds 1.15, recorded as an
  HC-06 R1/R2 elimination with the generator, command and build as the artifact.
- Profile-scope decisions: under Appendix B.1 `encode_wall_s` is a non-inferiority guard at the
  profile's T-03 band rather than a primary; both roles are pre-declared.

## 14. Expected negative results worth recording

- The frozen balanced dictionary training is the largest pack-time cost of balanced relative to
  fast, without a byte gain beyond band (links to EXP-CROSSFILE-003).
- Extreme's lookback search costs several band units of CPU for sub-band byte gains.
- A similarity alternative with better bytes fails OD-06 non-inferiority.
- Production's similarity or cohort search shows superlinear CPU on the colliding-bucket series
  (HC-06 defect at baseline).

## 15. Threats to validity

- WSL2 timing is virtualized (vCPU placement over P/E cores uncontrolled): soft cap MEDIUM; the
  host-side sampler and calibration bound the noise but not the bias.
- In-process timers exclude process start, which is correct for stage costs but not for CLI
  wall time (reported separately by the runner).
- Research planners run production stages through `research-internals` wrappers; codegen may
  differ from the shipped CLI (R1-06); relative comparisons among candidates in the same binary
  are unaffected to first order, and absolute CLI costs come from EXP-EVAL baselines.
- Survivor-set timing only: a candidate eliminated on bytes is never timed (intended, §13).
