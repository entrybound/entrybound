## 1. Question

(a) For CI artifact and cache hand-off of build trees, dependency trees and source checkouts, is the implemented CLI non-inferior to the incumbent CI cache pipeline (`tar` + `zstd --long=30`, the pipeline used by the GitHub Actions cache toolkit) on encode time, decode time, stored bytes and memory, and how much faster is partial restore of one subtree from an INDEXED archive than a full decode? (b) How much verification time do the verification designs of DEC-INT-028, DEC-INT-045 and DEC-LEG-009 cost on large artifacts: digest comparison before parsing versus full decoding verification, digest comparison versus strict re-import, byte re-export versus strict-import LAI comparison?

## 2. Hypotheses and falsification

- **H1 (CI encode).** On `st-v`, `ebound pack --profile fast` is `DISTINGUISHABLE_WORSE` than `tar | zstd -3 -T1 --long=30` on `encode_wall_s` at corpus level (predicted 1-4 band units of T-03 `fast`), and `NON_INFERIOR` or better on `artifact_bytes`. *Falsified* if encode is `NON_INFERIOR`, or if `artifact_bytes` is `DISTINGUISHABLE_WORSE`.
- **H2 (CI decode).** `ebound unpack` of a `fast` STREAM archive is `NON_INFERIOR` to `zstd -d | tar -x` on `decode_wall_s`. *Falsified* by `DISTINGUISHABLE_WORSE`.
- **H3 (partial restore).** Restoring one pre-registered subtree (≤ 5% of logical bytes) from an INDEXED archive with `ebound read` per entry is `DISTINGUISHABLE_BETTER` on `decode_wall_s` than full `zstd -d | tar -x` then selecting the subtree. *Falsified* if not `DISTINGUISHABLE_BETTER`.
- **H4 (digest-before-parse verification).** `sha256sum -c` of a published container digest is `DISTINGUISHABLE_BETTER` on `verify_wall_s` than `ebound verify` for medium and large artifacts. *Falsified* otherwise.
- **H5 (sidecar and receipt checks).** Digest comparison is `DISTINGUISHABLE_BETTER` on `verify_wall_s` than strict re-import for legacy artifacts, and strict-import LAI comparison is `DISTINGUISHABLE_WORSE` than byte re-export comparison. *Falsified* by the opposite verdicts.

## 7. Procedure and commands

Scripts in `research/tools/ecosystem/ci/` (to be written):

1. `prepare.sh` — build dev-HEAD `ebound` from the production workspace without `research-internals` (harness review R1-06); produce and store encoded artifacts per (candidate, item) once, outside timing, under `/root/eb-research/cache/eco008/<candidate>/<item_id>.*` for the decode, partial and verify specs; `pick_subtree.py`; publish digests, sidecars and receipts for the verify arm.
2. Timing runs in exclusive windows: `ebr run --timing --spec spec-encode.yaml`, then decode, partial, verify specs; `ebr verify-raw`; `ebr normalize`.
3. Adaptive rounds (§4.6): after the minimum rounds, `research/tools/ecosystem/ci/adaptive.py` computes per-item exact order-statistic intervals of median paired log ratios and schedules follow-up runs (`--run-id` suffix) for items whose half-width exceeds 0.5 × `log1p(r)` of each primary metric, up to the tier cap, never inspecting effect direction.
4. Decision analysis with the §14 item 2 tooling on the normalized outputs.

## 14. Held-out (Phase D placeholder)

## 15. Expected negative results worth recording

Single-threaded `ebound pack` losing clearly to multi-threaded incumbents in CI (informational arm) and to single-threaded zstd on encode; STREAM decode competitive; partial restore winning only when the subtree is small; full `ebound verify` far slower than a container digest check (which verifies less).

## 16. Threats to validity

`VIRTUALIZED` WSL timing (vCPU placement, VHDX over NTFS, warm host cache); Defender scanning of Windows-side host files does not affect WSL ext4 directly but host load does (host sampler); the CI cache toolkit's exact pipeline flags are taken from its source at a pinned tag (cited in `record.md` with path and line); partial-restore via per-entry `read` is process-per-entry and overstates cost versus an in-process API (EXP-ECO-020 reports the API form).

## 17. Cost estimate

About 45 h of exclusive quiet-window machine time (4 specs × 11-13 items × 3-5 candidates × up to 20-30 rounds plus warmups; medium items dominate); about 60 GB disk for stored encoded artifacts and scratch; agent effort scripted (runs), judgment only for analysis by an unexposed session.
